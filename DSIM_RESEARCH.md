## Verified source facts and implemented changes

- `package.json`: DSIM's 3D engine uses `@dimforge/rapier3d-deterministic-compat` 0.20.0. The browser edition now uses that exact package and version. This changes the underlying rigid-body library, not the whole DSIM application.
- `src/games/biobuzz/hive.ts` and `config.ts`: the pollen threshold by nectar count is `[8, 7, 6, 3, 1, 0]`. Adapted into `frontend/src/dsim-hive.js` with the required notice.
- `sim3d/hive3d.ts`: a detent holds the tray at a 30-degree stop until the table releases it. The dynamic tray swings on a revolute joint, with gravity, contact forces, angular damping and a minimum angular speed toward the opposite stop. BIOBUZZ now implements this structure with real colliding balls that remain visible in the tray and spill through its open outer end. Points accrue at the opposite stop, not at release. Membership is measured in the tilted tray's own frame.
- `config.ts` and `sim3d/bodies.ts`: DSIM uses estimated pollen mass 0.2 lb, nectar mass ratio 1.6, friction 0.6, bounce 0.55, angular damping 0.4, hive damping 4.466/s, and 13 lb tray + 6 lb ballast. The browser adopts these estimates and the tray's calculated center of mass and moment of inertia. Swing duration depends on load and contact positions; it is not asserted to equal DSIM's timing.
- `fieldDims.gen.ts` and `field-measurements.json`: the hives now have X-axis hinges at X ±12.75 inches and height 43.95 inches. The cells extend along the audience/rear axis, with a pitched roof, clear skins, colored ribs, closed back and open outer end. Their 11.75-inch depth and 20.141-inch width produce raised openings at 53.375–65.497 inches. The triangular support frame and panel collision shapes use the same geometry as the rendering. These original procedural parts replace the former open-top trays. **No DSIM/FIRST CAD mesh is copied**: DSIM's model README records unresolved redistribution permission for the CAD-derived assets.
- `presets.ts` and `config.ts`: the five added preset adaptations are StarterBot (tank/dumper), Pollinator (mecanum/turret/rear Box Tube), Forager (butterfly/dumper/two intakes), Skimmer (X-drive/twin turret) and Sniper (swerve/pollen turret/two intakes). Footprints and declared demo masses were converted from inches/pounds. StarterBot mass and drive/launcher performance remain estimates. Meshes are original simple procedural shapes.
- The builder changes drivetrain, launcher, front/back/both intake, Box Tube position, dimensions, mass, speed and firing interval independently for each alliance slot. It saves on the current device. Swerve has rate-limited pod steering; X-drive normalizes commands in omni wheel space; butterfly toggles holonomic/traction operation. Chassis forces approximate wheel traction; these are not full per-wheel motor/suspension models.
- Flowers now use original rectangular plates with bore openings, four green posts, a rear support and purple backstop, following the official drawing and DSIM measurements. Annular convex sectors form physical plate holes; eight contact strips approximate confinement between the posts. Balls stay dynamic, settle into a column, and fall when lower pollen is removed. A smaller middle bore sorts pollen from nectar, following DSIM's gameplay correction to its measured CAD bore. This is procedural collision geometry, not the imported CAD assembly.
- Normal intake retrieves bottom pollen when an intake faces the open retrieval side and has capacity. Box Tube placement releases a ball inside the top mouth; an actual shot through the top can also enter. Bottom/side intrusions cannot acquire flower membership. Scoring excludes pieces wholly below the middle plate and applies topmost-nectar ownership and bottommost-nectar bonus. G410 gates nectar placement at 1:00; pollen can be placed earlier. See `RULES.md` for the TU06 audit and remaining referee/physics boundaries.

## Rebuild appearance and controls

Rebuild was visually inspected for the flat, simple robot silhouettes, untextured bright balls, dark field, wire-like field perimeter and driver-station perspective. BIOBUZZ uses its own box/hopper/launcher models, flat shading and unlit ball materials. The browser driver camera now sits on the red alliance's left side. Manual holonomic W moves away from that station (+X), and D moves right (+Z), even while rotating. The desktop edition retains its original axes and Java gamepad contract.

Assisted browser shots now solve a lower ballistic arc with chassis-velocity compensation and much less artificial air drag. The original Rebuild physics implementation and coefficients remain unavailable from the inspected sources; no exact 1:1 physics or graphics claim is made.

## Edition boundary

The new DSIM physics, measured hive and flower geometry, TU06 match setup, five presets, builder and shift mode are in **browser Play**. The desktop Java runtime still uses JBullet, its original field model and ten presets. Fullscreen works in both editions. The public site still saves Java drafts only. Real FTC SDK execution is available in the separate desktop integration.
